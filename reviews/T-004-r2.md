## What the evaluator will trip over
1. A malformed `doc.list`/`buffer.text` call with a big array param floods the output (R2-D1).
2. A FIFO or device file in the root hangs `run`/`control` (R2-SG2).
3. Offsets are char indices; this appears only in `--schema` (R2-N4).
4. Symlinks are followed out of the root. This is now documented, and T-008 tracks it.
