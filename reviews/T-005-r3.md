### Follow-up candidates (for the director's follow-up task)
1. F19 — bind `merged` records to the queue's own record commit; otherwise the main-history anchor
   can be moved by any ledger commit.
2. F20 — sign-independent record commit, or report `merged` when only the record commit failed,
   so a successful merge is not reported as an error.
3. F21 — require `[lints] workspace = true` in every member, and flag in-source lint `allow`s in
   low tasks. Both silence the gate without being gate paths.
4. F22/F23 — tests for the record-commit pathspec and the identity fallback. Both protect
   behaviour that runs on every queue run.
5. F24 — let `ledger` ignore untracked drafts, or say so in the refusal hint, so drafting does
   not block the queue.
6. Round-2 reading — fixture projects under `tests/` are escalated to tier high; consider an
   exemption for `tests/fixtures/**` with no effect on the gate.
