## Differential run (my own driver and model)
- Generator: per seed 3–6 documents, each with an encoding drawn from `utf-8`, `utf-8-bom`,
  `utf-16le`, `utf-16be` (UTF-8 with and without a BOM; UTF-16 with its BOM) and a kind from LF,
  CRLF, CR, none, mixed (at least one LF and one CRLF plus random), empty, final break; lines of
  0–4 pieces from the alphabet: `a`, `Zq`, `7`, space, tab, `é`, `e`+U+0301, two U+0301 alone,
  U+200D, `👨`+U+200D+`👩`, `ffi`, `ﬃ` (U+FB03), `漢字`, `😀`, `שלום`, `سلام`, `٣`, `x y`.
- Steps (random): control `doc.open` (new and `already_open`), caret keys with none/`shift`/`ctrl`/
  both (with or without a `focus` on `Editor` first), clicks on `Editor` `at` a random row 0–3 and
  column 0–6, `focus` + typed text from the alphabet, `Enter`/`Backspace`/`Delete`, `ctrl+Z`/
  `ctrl+Y`, tab clicks, `Documents` clicks, `Close` clicks (clean and dirty), control `doc.close`,
  `cursor.set` (caret or selection, random offsets: some refused, `not_a_boundary` inside a CRLF
  among them), `doc.insert` of a piece or a break, `eol.normalize` with and without `to`,
  `doc.undo`, clicks on a bar node. Each control request is followed by exactly one frame.
- After every step: a snapshot, `doc.list`, and `cursor.get` + `doc.info` of every id that may be
  open. Model: current = last successful `doc.open` or tab/`Documents` switch; when it is no longer
  in `doc.list`, the highest open id; none open ⇒ no bar node. Expected: `Ln ` + (`line`+1),
  `Col ` + (`col`+1), `encoding`, `line_ending` + ` (mixed)` when mixed; each label exactly one
  node of role `label`, not focused, and at the default size `rows` = [value]. A mismatch prints
  seed, step and the step's requests.
- Runs on the reviewed binary: seeds 1–200 × 40 steps at 1280x800: 8,000 steps checked, 0
  mismatches; seeds 1001–1050 × 40 at `--size 800x320` and 2001–2050 × 40 at `--size 300x900`
  (values only, rows not required): 2,000 + 2,000 steps, 0 mismatches. Idle: seeds 1–40 × 25 steps
  plus one frame, with and without 600 more frames: 40 of 40 logs equal.
- Negative controls: the model with a 0-based column, and with the lowest open id as current,
  each flag all 5 seeds of 1–5; the mutant binaries R3, R5, R12, R13 flag 31, 60, 58, 53 of seeds
  1–60.
